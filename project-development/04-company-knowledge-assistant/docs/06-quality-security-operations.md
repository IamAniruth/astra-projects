# Quality, privacy, security and operations

Definition of done: concrete artifact or implementation, meaningful domain/integration checks, access and failure evidence, actual version identities, limitations and named reviewer. AI acceptance additionally requires independent support and citation evaluation on the release model/corpus. Mocks and upstream platform tests do not establish customer answer quality.

Enforce tenant, document and conversation grants in search, snippets, counts, citations, history, caches, feedback, gap dashboards and audit views. Test role/group revocation during generation, external-link recipients and follow-up turns. An administrator can configure the service without automatically receiving all source or employee-question content.

Content publication requires authorized ownership, audience/effective dates and review rules. Source deletion/withdrawal propagates through authoritative stores, indexes, prompts, cached answers, histories and gap evidence according to a documented policy. Preserve only justified audit metadata under restricted access. Restore and rollback must apply current tombstones/grants and cannot resurrect withdrawn policies.

Parse untrusted files in bounded workers and validate types, limits, source locators and prompt injection containment. Separate document content from system instructions. Diagnostic logs omit source text and personal questions by default; support access is scoped and auditable. Service processing and feedback permission do not imply model-training permission.

The employee experience distinguishes supported explanations, source conflicts, missing information and unavailable runtime. Answers are not approvals or binding employment/financial decisions. Link to the applicable policy and responsible owner for exceptions. This plan establishes no jurisdiction-specific policy or compliance requirements.

Operational qualification includes target-host/model/corpus capacity, bounded queues, cancellation, uncertain-outcome reconciliation, durable audit, process supervision, TLS, credential rotation, backup restore, incident ownership and rollback. Subscription seat revocation and identity changes must take effect across caches and sessions.

Release requires accepted discovery/feasibility, qualified supported sources, independent answer/freshness/access evidence, authorized gap analytics, restore/revocation drills, pilot outcomes and applicable Astra blockers closed. Connector, language and corpus expansion each need separate qualification.
