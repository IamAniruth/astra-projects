# Invoice processing roadmap

All work is Planned. Three sprints per Program Increment follow project 01. There are 24 planned sprints: 18 original conditional core, three optional improvement and three administration. At two weeks per sprint, core plus administration represents 42 nominal sequential weeks, plus six optional weeks. This organizes scope, not a delivery commitment. Re-estimate overlap with existing services and actual staffing after discovery/feasibility.

| PI | Outcome | Sprints |
|---|---|---|
| [PI-01](PI-01-foundation/README.md) | Investigation and secure foundation | S01-S03 |
| [PI-02](PI-02-document-intake/README.md) | Document intake and processing | S04-S06 |
| [PI-03](PI-03-extraction-validation/README.md) | Extraction and deterministic validation | S07-S09 |
| [PI-04](PI-04-review-export/README.md) | Review, approval and exports | S10-S12 |
| [PI-05](PI-05-commercial-readiness/README.md) | Commercial and operational readiness | S13-S15 |
| [PI-06](PI-06-pilot-launch/README.md) | Pilot qualification and launch | S16-S18 |
| [PI-07](PI-07-controlled-improvement/README.md) | Optional controlled improvement | S19-S21 |
| [PI-08](PI-08-admin-panel/README.md) | Client/workspace administration and invoice governance | S22-S24 |

S01 is go/narrow/stop; S02 is feasibility; S12 is the internal reviewed-export milestone; S18 is conditional launch. Internal demos need not have checkout, but paid pilots require applicable commercial/operational readiness. PI-07 is optional.

PI-08 numbering does not require waiting until after PI-07 or launch. S22 consumes S03/S13; S23 consumes S04-S12 with S14 profile qualification; S24 consumes S06/S13-S15. Earlier sprints own domain services and PI-08 owns admin UI/orchestration and integrated verification. Schedule required controls before the S17 paid pilot; S18 includes final admin evidence. S16-S18 quality/pilot results may display as pending while admin development proceeds, avoiding circular entry gates.
