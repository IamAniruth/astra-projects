# Product scope and investigation gates

Buyer hypothesis: equipment service firms or maintenance teams with repeated manual lookup work. Choose one equipment family and one reachable team first. Interview technicians, document owners, service experts and the budget owner. Record existing search time, recurring questions, version confusion, escalation paths and document onboarding effort.

Initial journey: select site and equipment identity -> ask question or enter fault code -> receive applicable passages or a source-linked answer -> open cited page -> follow an approved troubleshooting branch or ask an expert. Screens: equipment selector, manual library, approval/version review, question workspace, source viewer, guided procedure, escalation queue, feedback and administration.

Equipment identity includes manufacturer, model, variant and applicable serial/firmware ranges where relevant. Do not guess these from a symptom. Clarify unresolved applicability before giving procedural guidance. Unknown equipment may permit clearly labelled document discovery, but cannot silently select a repair procedure.

Start with one supported language and readable text PDFs; qualify printed scans separately. Diagrams, schematics, tables with uncertain cell structure, handwriting, videos and translated procedures require their own evidence before enabling interpretation. Show original pages where accessible; a citation to a drawing does not establish that the model understood it.

Only explicitly approved, applicable and accessible revisions support answers. Raw service notes and expert replies are not approved procedures. New notes require document-owner review before publication. An older revision can remain valid for an older serial range; upload date alone cannot determine applicability.

S01 go/narrow/stop needs buyer access, permitted manuals, a named domain reviewer, baseline metrics and draft numeric success thresholds. S02 requires real retrieval/answer evidence using a selected checkpoint, a viable parsing path and authenticated isolation design. Failure narrows to approved passage search, defers synthesis or stops the build; a search-only demonstration cannot count as an AI answer release.

Commercial hypothesis from the opportunity document: per-site or team subscription plus document onboarding. Validate willingness to pay against actual onboarding, runtime and support costs. No demand, pricing or productivity improvement is established here.
