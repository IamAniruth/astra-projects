# Quality, safety, security and operations

Definition of done: implementation or discovery artifact, meaningful domain/integration checks, failure/access evidence, actual version identities, limitations and named reviewer. AI acceptance requires independent source-support and applicability evaluation on the selected checkpoint. Platform tests and mocks cannot qualify equipment guidance.

The product must distinguish source lookup from an instruction to perform work. Show approved prerequisites, required qualifications and stop conditions as part of the procedure. If evidence is incomplete or conflicting, provide relevant approved passages and escalation, not invented repair advice. Product release requires an equipment-domain reviewer to accept the supported procedure scope; this document supplies no maintenance instructions or regulatory certification.

Cover tenant/site/document isolation across search, answer history, citation viewers, caches, job status and escalations. Test source withdrawal, role revocation and asset-context changes during an in-flight query. Previously generated answers must not bypass current access or freshness checks. Version comparisons may remain in an authorized administrative view while superseded guidance is blocked from current answers.

Parse untrusted documents in a bounded environment. Preserve numbers, units, warnings and diagrams without claiming unqualified interpretation. Verify injection containment and malicious attachment handling. Support accessible source navigation and clear distinctions between no evidence, wrong equipment, denied access and runtime failure without leaking inaccessible source titles.

Retention/deletion covers originals, OCR, chunks, indexes, cached prompts/answers and source-bearing escalation records, with backup policy and audit metadata documented. Service feedback is not training consent. Rebuild/restore must respect withdrawal tombstones and not republish retracted sources.

Operations qualification: target-host capacity with actual corpus/model, queue/budget limits, remote job reconciliation, process supervision, TLS, credentials, durable audit, restore drills, index rollback and support ownership. A rollback cannot resurrect withdrawn manuals or revoked permissions. Paid pilots require applicable commercial and operational readiness.

Release gates: discovery and feasibility, approved source corpus and asset applicability, independent answer/citation/safety checks, access and withdrawal checks, operational recovery, observed pilot outcomes and applicable Astra dependencies. Diagrams, languages and new equipment families remain disabled until separately qualified.
