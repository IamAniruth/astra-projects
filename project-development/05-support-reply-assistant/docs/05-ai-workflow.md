# Case summaries, source-supported drafts and evaluation

Pipeline: authenticate -> validate case context/version -> build attributable timeline -> summarize with unresolved facts -> retrieve permitted current knowledge -> obtain authorized live facts if configured -> draft -> check support/disclosure/tone -> agent edits and approves -> copy/export approved public text.

Preserve who said what and when. Separate reported symptoms, verified information, prior advice, attempted steps and still-open questions. Quoted histories and imported retries must not duplicate messages or invert chronology. Truncation is explicit; mandatory recent messages, privacy flags and outstanding commitments cannot disappear through compression. If context is too large, request a bounded case segment or abstain.

Retrieve approved sources by product/version, language and policy applicability after access filtering. Raw historical tickets are neither policy nor current operational truth. Prefer current approved knowledge; use only reviewed redacted examples for style or reusable guidance, without other customers' identifiers, facts or commitments.

Customer text and attachments are untrusted data. They cannot instruct the assistant to disclose secrets, bypass review or call business actions. Validate citation resolution independently from whether the cited material supports the reply. A customer saying a refund was approved is not evidence the business approved or processed it.

Live facts come only from an authorized system query bound to the correct customer/account/order, with observed time and freshness requirements. Snapshot expiry or lookup failure creates an agent-visible blocker. Without a connector, omit unverified assertions and ask the agent to check. Never fill placeholders with guessed delivery dates, status or balances.

Drafts use approved tone and language while preserving qualifications and policy exceptions. Do not promise compensation, admit actions were completed or assert an SLA/entitlement without applicable evidence and authority. Internal notes may inform routing but are not automatically customer-disclosable. Keep the internal evidence view separate from the final reply; allowlist public references and scan for internal-only content and unrelated customer data.

Agent review shows supporting passages, disputed facts, missing checks and sensitive/disallowed content. Agent edits are versioned and rechecked; approval applies to exact draft/case/source/fact versions. A new message or changed policy invalidates approval. Export rechecks freshness and contains only the approved reply. No autonomous send occurs.

Evaluation corpus: permitted articles/tickets plus synthetic edge cases, split by customer/case thread and issue/template family to prevent leakage. Include quoted histories, private notes, similar customer names, wrong orders, contradictory messages, changed policies, unresolved promised actions, malicious attachments, long cases, unsupported questions and group/case access revocation.

Pre-register numeric thresholds and sample minimums with a support reviewer. Measure summary factuality/completeness/attribution, draft factual support, policy adherence, disclosure errors, critical omissions, edit distance/correction types, acceptance rate, total handling time including review, latency and cost. Customer satisfaction requires real permitted downstream outcomes and an attribution caveat; do not invent CSAT from offline draft ratings. Report acceptance alongside factual corrections, not as a quality proxy. Any cross-customer disclosure or critical unsupported customer commitment observed in release evaluation blocks release until fixed and re-evaluated. No results are claimed yet.
